# Product Strategy and MVP Scope

## Value proposition

TEKMESIS transforms a researchable personal question into a transparent, source-linked Premium Evidence Report.

Users pay for:
- formulation of a researchable question
- source selection
- study screening
- structured comparison
- evidence synthesis
- explanation of uncertainty
- practical orientation
- source traceability
- premium presentation

They do not pay merely for links to publicly available studies.

## Target user

Primary:

**Wissbegierige Privatpersonen, die fundierte, evidenzbasierte Antworten auf persönliche Fragen und Entscheidungen im Alltag suchen.**

Typical characteristics:
- non-specialists
- interested in making better-informed decisions
- dissatisfied with contradictory internet information
- value understandable explanations and visible sources
- willing to pay for a structured synthesis

- community
- personalized diagnosis
